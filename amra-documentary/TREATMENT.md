# The Fly: The Escape of Mohamed Amra (treatment)

Style: **Paper-cut Lightbox** (`paper-lantern`), Lemo-Opuscar library. The user's production script (11 scenes + credits) is binding for structure, narration, title cards, walking animation and on-screen text. This treatment decides how the style carries it.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One box per scene.** Each scene is its own lightbox stack with its own colour ladder; chapter cards are the lamp switching off and on.
2. **One lamp, the scene inside changes.** A single locked box (the toll booth) whose sheets are swapped out for every place, light being the only transition.
3. **A vertical stack.** The camera tilts down through the layers (sky, city, prison, road) as the story descends.

**Chosen: 1, bookended by 2.** The script moves place by place with chapter cards, and changing the ladder is how this style says "another place". The toll booth (Scenes 1, 6, 11) is the same box each time, so the ending reads as the same place, now quiet. A vertical stack (3) would impose a metaphor the script doesn't ask for.

## 2. Logline and arc

A prisoner is freed from a van at a Normandy toll booth at the cost of two officers' lives; nine months later he is found in Bucharest. Setup (Rouen, record, network, prison) → turn (Incarville, 11:00) → aftermath (the fallen, the hunt, the arrest) → ending (the toll booth quiet again, in memory of the two men).

## 3. Benchmark

- Lotte Reiniger's silhouette films; layered shadow-box lamps. **Learn:** profile acting by jointed cut-outs, depth from a value ladder, figures read by silhouette alone. **Don't take:** any figure, frame or design.
- Investigative documentary pacing (slow pushes, a held aftermath frame, numbers given plainly). **Learn:** restraint; no re-enactment of violence. **Don't take:** layouts or graphics.

## 4. Colour

Cold ladders throughout (night blue / winter grey), for example `#0a1330` → `#6a86c2` → `#3f5a92` → `#0e1532`. The backlight stays the style's warm glow, kept low so the cold ladder dominates. **One accent: blue emergency light**, held back until after the attack (Scenes 6 and 11). Wreaths and candles in Scene 7 take the only warm light.

## 5. Shot list, beat sheet, cue map, sound table, type

Written scene by scene after the narration is recorded (the voice lengths set the grid). Built after the style test (Scene 2 walk) is approved.

## 6. Violence and likeness

Silhouettes only: figures are flat dark sheets with no features, so no face is ever drawn. No weapons, gunfire, blood or bodies. Scene 6 cuts to black at the moment of the attack.
